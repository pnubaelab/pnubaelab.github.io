Professor Hyerim Bae of Pusan National University has been selected as a recipient of the **37th Baekam Paper Award(백암논문상)** from the Korean Institute of Industrial Engineers(산업공학회).


{% quote 추정호2025장비 %}
The award recognizes his paper, “장비의 고장 단계 함수를 이용한 상태 지표 추출”, published in the Journal of the Korean Institute of Industrial Engineers.
{% endquote %}

The Baekam Paper Award honors authors of outstanding papers published in the journal over the past three years, recognizing both scholarly merit and practical value for industrial applications.


Congratulations to Professor Bae on this recognition!
