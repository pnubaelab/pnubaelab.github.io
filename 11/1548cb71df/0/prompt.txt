**Health indicator learning with high-dimensional shape constraint function: mitigating information loss in one-dimensional autoencoder latent spaces**   [![Order a copy of this article](https://www.inderscience.com/images/basket.gif)](mailto:subs@inderscience.com)
*by Hanbyeol Park, Minseop Kim, Dohee Kim, Yunkyung Park, Hyerim Bae *
**Abstract**: Autoencoders are widely used for health indicator (HI) extraction. Traditional supervised learning-based methods for constructing HIs assume equipment degradation states through a shape constraint function (SCF). In this process, autoencoders based on SCF exhibit a unique structure in which the latent space is constrained to a one-dimensional representation. This limitation leads to information loss, ultimately impairing the performance of HI extraction. This study proposes high-dimensional SCF (HSCF) as a redefined degradation function in higher dimensions. Furthermore, a new training methodology, denoted health indicator learning (HIL), is introduced. HIL comprises two stages: the training process and transformation of high-dimensional latent vectors into one-dimensional HIs. By leveraging HIL, Copyright ? 20XX Inderscience Enterprises Ltd. 2 H. Park et al. the autoencoder's latent space is expanded, thereby reducing information loss and preserving essential data features. HIL improves by 0.0238 on four PRONOSTIA test sets and by 0.0112 on Korean weapon system operational test sets versus state-of-the-art baselines. [Received 23 July 2025; Accepted 26 November 2025]
